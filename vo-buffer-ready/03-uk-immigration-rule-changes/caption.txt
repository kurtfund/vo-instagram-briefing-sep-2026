The UK is introducing several immigration rule changes from October 2026 — on different dates.

Eligible Skilled Worker visa holders who are recognised victims of modern slavery can, with a positive Conclusive Grounds decision, work for another employer for the remainder of their current permission.

The Victim of Domestic Abuse route is expanding to cover certain adult dependent children.

Visitor, Student and Child Student rules are being updated to support the UK’s participation in Erasmus+. This is not a new Student visa route.

Suitability rules are being clarified around immigration breaches, including absconding and failing to report while on immigration bail.

Key dates:
8 October 2026 — most changes under HC 584
29 October 2026 — certain religious worker route changes
30 November 2026 — Student visa maintenance funds increase
9 December 2026 — certain EU Settlement Scheme biometric changes

Always check the latest GOV.UK guidance before you act.
