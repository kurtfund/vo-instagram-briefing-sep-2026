Europe’s entry rules are changing — and EES and ETIAS are not the same thing.

EES is already here. It is Europe’s digital border system for non-EU travellers on short stays. Passport, face and fingerprints are recorded at the border instead of stamps. There is no EES application to buy before you travel.

ETIAS is different. It will be a pre-travel authorisation for travellers from visa-exempt countries. It is not a visa. You do not need to apply for ETIAS right now. Official EU guidance currently points to a 2027 launch, with several months’ notice before it starts.
